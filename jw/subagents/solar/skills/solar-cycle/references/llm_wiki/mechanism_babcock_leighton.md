The Babcock-Leighton (BL) mechanism is a widely accepted framework for the solar dynamo. It consists of two main steps:

1. Poloidal-to-toroidal field conversion: differential rotation shears a poloidal magnetic field into a toroidal field, which erupts as sunspot pairs (Babcock process).
2. Toroidal-to-poloidal field regeneration: decay of tilted active regions disperses magnetic flux, and meridional circulation transports it to the poles, reversing the polar field (Leighton process).

Implications for prediction:
- The strength of the polar field near cycle minimum is thought to be a precursor of the next cycle's amplitude (polar-field precursor hypothesis).
- The tilt angle distribution of sunspot groups affects flux cancellation and polar-field buildup.

Caveats:
- The BL mechanism is a surface-flux-transport model; the deep-seated dynamo may have additional components.
- Polar-field records are short, so precursor-based predictions have limited statistical power.

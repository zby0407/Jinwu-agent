# Canonical Entry Review Checklist

## Scope

- [ ] The entry directly supports a task or Agent responsibility in the design.
- [ ] It is compact model-facing knowledge, not copied textbook prose.
- [ ] Its type and id follow the seven-type project schema.
- [ ] `valid_range` is specific enough to constrain downstream use.

## Scientific content

- [ ] Observation, proxy, derived statistic, model result, and mechanism claim
      are clearly distinguished.
- [ ] A statistical association is not presented as mechanism proof.
- [ ] Numerical values, units, formulas, and thresholds have authoritative
      sources.
- [ ] Formula and table content has been checked against the original page, not
      only OCR text.
- [ ] Competing explanations, counterevidence, or failure conditions are
      retained.
- [ ] Small-sample, calibration, drift, and coverage limitations are explicit.

## Project behavior

- [ ] The entry says which task and Agent consume it.
- [ ] The entry prevents at least one concrete failure mode or supports one
      concrete decision.
- [ ] Related ids exist or are explicitly cataloged as planned.
- [ ] The entry does not authorize deterministic Cycle 26 forecasting.
- [ ] Flare/event knowledge is not substituted for long-term cycle evidence.

## Provenance and lifecycle

- [ ] Source title, edition/year, chapter/page, or DOI/URL is recorded.
- [ ] Each material content field can be traced to a supporting source.
- [ ] Candidate-to-canonical promotion reason is recorded.
- [ ] Conflicts are logged rather than silently harmonized.
- [ ] Reviewer and last-reviewed date are recorded.
- [ ] Redistribution of source text is permitted, or only compact original
      synthesis and local source locators are retained.

## Acceptance

- [ ] A second reader can identify what the entry supports.
- [ ] A second reader can identify what the entry does not support.
- [ ] Removing the entry would measurably weaken at least one representative
      research scenario.

## claim

Precursor methods for solar cycle amplitude prediction have consistently outperformed extrapolation methods over recent solar cycles. The polar field precursor is the single most reliable predictor, yielding predictions consistently in the right range. Prediction methods fall into three groups: precursor methods, model-based approaches, and extrapolation methods.

## supporting_evidence

The polar field precursor has yielded predictions consistently in the right range during the past few solar cycles. Model-based forecasts are gaining community confidence despite lacking a long proven record. Some extrapolation methods may still be worth further study.
